0. The Point (CANONICAL — every other text about br_id_ge derives from this, sized down)

ONE LINE.
br_id_ge (կամուրջ, "bridge") is how the real world enters di.iiii — Gevorg Aram
Grigoryan's spatial platform — and becomes shared space.

ONE PARAGRAPH.
Everything real that wants to live in di.iiii crosses this bridge, through one of
three doors. Places enter as 3D scans (depth cameras, LiDAR). People enter through
a short rite: you cross a bridge built of glowing Armenian letters and answer two
questions — who are you, and what do you carry? Artists enter through an open jam,
curated in by Gevorg. Every entry leaves a permanent mark — a digitalkar (kar is
Armenian for stone): not a monument, a core. The sun baked this rock once; the
same mineral sat in water a few billion years and stood up as us, and now we make
light again. The field of cores is never erased. And the bridge runs both ways:
what is built inside di.iiii can be carried back out across it. br_id_ge opens
publicly at Notations #2 (July 20 – August 2, 2026, State Philharmonia, Yerevan,
hosted by hosq) and keeps growing after it.

WHAT MUST STAY TRUE — and what checks it. The festival opened with a closed field:
`openInscriptions` sat false, every inscription was refused, and the rite played its
closing lines unchanged, so nine days of visitors left nothing behind. The three
prerequisites were written down in this file and two of them silently failed. The
lesson is not "be more careful" — it is that a written intention is not a mechanism.

3. The Notation Concept — and the bridge as a score (DECIDED)

- A notation/score is "sound and text, movement and light, architecture and
  mistake, silence and interaction."
- Source material is treated as active: dissected, translated, recomposed across
  disciplines (e.g. Avet Terterian's Symphony No. 3). Artists "compose through
  touch, speech, gesture, movement, editing, rhythm, attention."
- Vocabulary spans code/algorithms and ancient systems — notably **Khaz**, the
  Armenian graphic neumes used as scores for acoustic instruments.
- **The bridge IS a score, and the visitor performs it.** This is no longer an open
  question: it is the piece's connection to the room it stands in. The letter bridge
  is read left to right in five timed acts, paced to a drone — that is a score being
  walked. Khaz notated how a voice should move through a text; the bridge notates
  how a body should move through a crossing. At a festival called Notations, this is
  the strongest card the work holds and it is played, not left curatorial.

4. Core Concepts (canonical vocabulary — keep these terms)

- "digital rite of transmission" — keeping identity continuous across rupture and
  distance requires digital rites. The foundational thesis.
- "br_ID_ge" — a bridge with identity at its structural center.
- The Keeper — "keeper of cultural memory / witness": strictly an archivist that
  asks "Who are you, and what do you carry across the bridge?" — not a spiritual
  entity. Since 2026-07-13 it can be a real local LLM running on site (layer 1 of
  the rig); when it is silent the scripted lines stand.
- **"digitalkar"** — the digital stone (kar = քար, Armenian for stone). The canonical
  name of what a crossing leaves behind: the visitor's name in rigid Erkatagir, their
  chosen word in decorative Trchnagir.

  **Not a monument. A core.** The sun baked this rock once already; the same mineral
  sat in water for a few billion years and stood up as us. We are cooled stone that
  learned to manipulate light — and the rite runs that whole arc in six minutes,
  backwards: the lamp gathers light, the light arrives as letters, and it settles
  into stone again. Every crossing adds one core to the field.

  RETIRED (2026-07-29): the digitalkar does **not** descend from the khachkar, and the
  earlier "deep lineage, felt not spoken" note is withdrawn. A khachkar is a memorial —
  it points backwards, it is grave-adjacent, and it belongs to a living faith that will
  read it its own way. This work is about continuation, not commemoration; the khachkar
  was quietly arguing the opposite. The word stays out of every surface, as before —
  but now because it is not the ancestor, not because it is a secret one.

- **Light is the medium** (see §6).
- Communitas — the goal of the ritual; the solo rite resolves into a shared,
  persistent field (Act V "Echo" + live co-presence mesh).
- The five acts: Threshold → Crossing → Witness → Inscription → Echo.

5. Research → Practice (what the research commits the piece to)

- Rites of passage need separation/sensory reduction → Act I strips the UI to a
  dark void lit by a single gantegh lamp.
- "Kinetic Refresh" (unconscious swipe-for-novelty) can be redirected into
  conscious transmission → no transactional UI; progress is breathing-paced,
  synchronized with the duduk drone.
- Stone inscription traditions demand continuation, users want semantic agency →
  the digitalkar carries both (name + chosen word).
- "Passive Informational Drift" isolates → the ending reveals the shared field of
  cores (occlusion 100% → 0%): the diaspora made visible.
- MSDF text rendering (multi-channel signed distance fields) keeps Erkatagir and
  Trchnagir edges clean in WebGL at high frame rates — the technical basis of the
  letter bridge.
- Autoethnography in HCI: the artist's insider position in the Armenian diaspora
  is generative data, not bias.
- The permanence promised to a visitor must be a real property of the system, not a
  sentence in Act V. It is: inscriptions are append-only *by construction* — the
  server builds the op itself, and nothing can update or delete one. This is the same
  non-negotiable that governs di.iiii's op-log. The artwork and the platform are one
  idea in two languages.

6. Light — the medium (replaces the old "Void" palette section)

- **The sun is the original programmer of atmosphere.** A high sun makes a room tense;
  a low one makes it warm and safe. We are wired to read light before we read content.
  We never see things — only the light coming off them. The light IS the environment.
- **The screen is emissive, not reflective.** A painting waits for a lamp. A projector
  or LED wall shoots photons straight into the retina. In a dark room the screen is not
  showing an image — it has become the sun of that room, and everyone in it is lit by
  the work. Make it red and the room feels the heat.
- **Code is the physics of that light.** A shader is arithmetic describing how a beam
  scatters and lands. To write one is to teach a machine the rules of nature, then bend
  them: this light throbs to the kick, this sunset turns with a singer's pitch.
- **Why this piece already is that.** The rite's first instruction is "you are the lamp."
  The letters are additively blended — they emit, they do not reflect. The scan takes
  real photons off a camera sensor and re-emits them as Armenian script. The venue ask
  follows from it: a dark room and walls as the surface, because the work is not hung
  in the room, it replaces the room's sun.
- Design tokens (canonical, identical on every surface except the rite and v.oooooo,
  which are the artwork's own face): void `#05080d` · panel `#0a1018` · line `#1b2634` ·
  prose `#c3d0dc` (Outfit 200/300) · mono chrome (Space Mono, lowercase, letterspaced) ·
  **cyan `#00e5ff`** = di.iiii/live/links · **crimson `#ff2a5f`** = the real/notation/drafts ·
  **amber `#ffb347`** = gantegh lamp/attention/⚠/CTA · **stone `#cdb98f`** = the core,
  Armenian letterforms (volcanic tuff — cooled magma, which is the concept, literally) ·
  dim `#5f6b7a` · white = the stitch. Colors are concept actors, not decoration.
