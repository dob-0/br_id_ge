0. The Point (canonical concept — everything below serves this)

- dob (Gevorg Aram Grigoryan) builds **di.iiii** — the browser-native XR platform.
- **br_id_ge is di.iiii's portal and collaborative process lab, framed by
  notation**: the named process by which the real world enters the platform and
  becomes shared space. It is where everyone can come to the builder.
- The flow: **reality → scan → notation → space → connection.**
  · places enter as scans (depth cameras, LiDAR — the scanner array)
  · people enter through the rite's questions ("who are you and what do you carry")
  · artists enter through curation — their notations bridged in as new nodes
- The portal is two-way and **recursive**: perform the ritual in br_id_ge and the
  result lives on in di.iiii (the inscription becomes a persistent object in shared
  space); build in di.iiii and carry it back across the bridge as what you carry.
  Each crossing is a new horizon; the loop repeats and deepens — the same recursion
  di.iiii's node model is built on.
- The rite (v.oooooo 2) is the portal performing itself — the demonstration piece.
  The inscription field is the portal's memory: append-only, never erased.
- The open visual jam is the portal open to artists: **Gevorg curates via
  br_id_ge.** An artist joining the jam is being bridged in, not exhibited beside.
- br_id_ge starts publicly at **Notations #2** — and does not end there; it is a
  standing practice that grows node by node.

3. The Notation Concept

- A notation/score is "sound and text, movement and light, architecture and
  mistake, silence and interaction."
- Source material is treated as active: dissected, translated, recomposed across
  disciplines (e.g. Avet Terterian's Symphony No. 3). Artists "compose through
  touch, speech, gesture, movement, editing, rhythm, attention."
- Vocabulary spans code/algorithms and ancient systems — notably Khaz, the
  Armenian graphic neumes used as scores for acoustic instruments. Khaz is the
  natural hinge between the laboratory's notation practice and the rite's bridge
  of Armenian letters: the bridge can be read as a score the visitor performs.

4. Core Concepts (canonical vocabulary — keep these terms)

- "digital rite of transmission" — keeping identity continuous across rupture and
  distance requires digital rites. The foundational thesis.
- "br_ID_ge" — a bridge with identity at its structural center.
- The Keeper — "keeper of cultural memory / witness": strictly an archivist that
  asks "Who are you, and what do you carry across the bridge?" — not a spiritual
  entity.
- "Epigraphic-Semantic Hybrid" — the dual-typography khachkar: the visitor's name
  in rigid Erkatagir on the base, their chosen word in decorative Trchnagir woven
  through the cross.
- Communitas — the goal of the ritual; the solo rite resolves into a shared,
  persistent field (Act V "Echo" + live co-presence mesh).
- The five acts: Threshold → Crossing → Witness → Inscription → Echo.

5. Research → Practice (what the research commits the piece to)

- Rites of passage need separation/sensory reduction → Act I strips the UI to a
  dark void lit by a single gantegh lamp.
- "Kinetic Refresh" (unconscious swipe-for-novelty) can be redirected into
  conscious transmission → no transactional UI; progress is breathing-paced,
  synchronized with the duduk drone.
- Khachkars demand genealogical continuation, users want semantic agency → the
  Epigraphic-Semantic Hybrid stone (name + chosen word).
- "Passive Informational Drift" isolates → the ending reveals the shared field of
  inscribed khachkars (occlusion 100% → 0%): the diaspora made visible.
- MSDF text rendering (multi-channel signed distance fields) keeps Erkatagir and
  Trchnagir edges clean in WebGL at high frame rates — the technical basis of the
  letter bridge.
- Autoethnography in HCI: the artist's insider position in the Armenian diaspora
  is generative data, not bias.
